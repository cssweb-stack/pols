Advising & Support

Undergraduate students are encouraged to seek advice from the current Undergraduate Chair , and they can also seek advice from any permanent POLS faculty member. All Political Science majors are also assigned to a faculty member who can help them navigate the department and talk with them about capstone courses and options beyond graduation. If you are a POLS major and unsure who your faculty mentor is, please contact the current Undergraduate Chair  or email polisci@hawaii.edu .  Students who wish to make an appointment with a faculty member should email or call them directly. 

Next Steps

Students are welcome to email polisci@hawaii.edu to obtain information on any of the following items:

  • Minoring in POLS
  • Majoring in POLS, including declaring a major
  • The BAM program
  • Which POLS courses will be offered in the upcoming semester
