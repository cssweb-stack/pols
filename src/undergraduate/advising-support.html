Advising & Support

Advising helps undergraduate students explore their options, plan their studies, and work toward graduation. Students can seek guidance on Political Science programs and courses, as well as general education requirements.

What Can We Help With?

Contact polungrd@hawaii.edu for undergraduate Political Science guidance, including:

  • Courses and upcoming course offerings
  • The Political Science minor
  • The Political Science major, including declaring a major
  • The BA/MA Pathway (BAM)
  • The Honors Track
  • The Law and Society certificate
  • Internship and scholarship opportunities
  • Capstone options

Faculty Mentors

All Political Science majors are assigned a faculty mentor who can help them navigate the department, explore capstone courses, and discuss options beyond graduation. Students who are unsure who their mentor is should contact the Undergraduate Chair.

Political Science majors may also seek advice from any permanent POLS faculty member. To arrange an appointment, email or call the faculty member directly.

Students can plan/track degree progress using Program Sheet & Academic Plan and STAR Degree Check.
