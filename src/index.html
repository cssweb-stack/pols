Political Science at the University of Hawaiʻi

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Political Science within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. The department focuses on the study of political power. A genuinely international department, its diverse students and faculty embody UH’s mission to be a “globally-connected Hawaiian place of learning.”

The department offers small student-centered classes that help students become critical thinkers, problem solvers, and effective communicators. Students learn how political power shapes and is shaped by individuals, communities, social movements, corporations, nations, and other forces. The curriculum develops knowledge and skills valued by professional employers, preparing graduates for careers in journalism, government, law, education, research, and other fields.
