Specializations

Our six specializations offer different ways to explore power, political life, and change. Through a range of perspectives and methods, students examine political ideas, institutions, and relationships in Hawaiʻi and around the world.

{% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

Alternative Futures

Explore how societies change and how different political futures might take shape. This specialization examines the forces driving transformation and develops alternative visions of what lies ahead.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

Comparative Politics

Examine how power, institutions, and political change operate across societies. Topics include democracy, development, identity, social movements, and human rights, with particular strengths in the Asia-Pacific region.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

Indigenous Politics

Study Indigenous peoples, lands, and political life through critical inquiry and community partnerships. The Indigenous Politics Program connects scholarship and practice, with particular attention to Hawaiʻi, Oceania, and Asia.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

International Relations

Explore the forces shaping relations across borders, from war and diplomacy to migration and the environment. This specialization brings a range of approaches to understanding international politics and global challenges.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

Law and Policy

Examine law and public policy in relation to society, culture, and power. Courses explore legal theory, constitutional law, public policy, and American politics through a variety of methods and perspectives.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 500, 500, sidebarClass="sidebar-left-med") %}

Political Theory

Explore the ideas and conventions that shape political life. Drawing on philosophy, literature, history, and other fields, this specialization brings feminist, queer, Indigenous, and critical race perspectives to political inquiry.

{% endcall %}
